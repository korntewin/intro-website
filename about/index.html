HOW I APPROACH ENGINEERING

A few working principles.

01 /

Measure first.

Start with profiles, traces, and reproducible benchmarks. Intuition is a hypothesis, not an answer.

02 /

Understand the machine.

Threads, memory, scheduling, and data layout often matter just as much as the model.

03 /

Make it operable.

Reliable deployments, clear boundaries, and useful observability are features too.
